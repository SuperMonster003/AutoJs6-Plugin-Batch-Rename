## Pinyin dictionary

The APK resources `pinyin/chars.txt`, `pinyin/phrases.txt` and `pinyin/t2s.txt` (the "Chinese characters to pinyin"
cleanup and the `${pinyin}` variable) are generated by `.python/generate_pinyin_dictionary.py` from the files below,
kept unchanged in `third_party/` with their license texts:

| Data | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Readings of single characters (`pinyin.txt`) | pinyin-data by mozillazg (https://github.com/mozillazg/pinyin-data), commit `9193766` | MIT |
| Readings of phrases (`pinyin.txt`, without `large_pinyin.txt`) | phrase-pinyin-data by mozillazg (https://github.com/mozillazg/phrase-pinyin-data), commit `cee0ed6` | MIT |
| Traditional to simplified characters (`TSCharacters.txt`) | OpenCC (https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC) `ver.1.4.2` | Apache-2.0 |

The APK carries the three license texts in `pinyin/NOTICE.txt`. Pinned commits and SHA-256 digests are recorded in
`third_party/README.md`. The corrections in `.python/pinyin_overwrite.txt` belong to this repository (MPL-2.0).
